The Frog at the Bottom of the Well
Hello, little friends! If you sat at the bottom of a well your whole life and looked up, would the sky look big or small? Long ago, there was a frog who believed the sky was only as big as a pot lid. Today, Huy Tells Tales will tell you the fable of the Frog at the Bottom of the Well.
Once upon a time, in a village beside the Red River, there were green bamboo hedges all around, a big banyan tree, and a temple house with a curved roof. In a corner of the village stood an old well, its walls covered in green moss. It had been a long time since anyone came there to draw water.
At the bottom of the well lived a little Frog with his friend Crab, some Snails, and a few tiny tree frogs. Every morning, Frog hopped onto the rock in the middle of the well and called out as loudly as he could. Frog (happily): "Ribbit! Ribbit! This morning I woke up earliest in the whole well again!"
His croak bounced off the walls of the well and rang out, deep and loud, sounding very grand. Frog (proudly): "Did you hear that? My voice echoes across the whole world!" Crab (worried): "Frog, that is only the walls of the well sending your voice back." Frog (shouting): "No! It is because I am the greatest!"
Frog looked up. Through the round mouth of the well, he saw only a small patch of blue sky. Frog (laughing): "The sky is only as big as a pot lid. I can see the whole sky!"
Frog puffed out his chest and his cheeks, and looked down at his little friends around him. Frog (firmly): "I am the king of the whole wide world! Crab, Snails, bow to me at once!" Crab (scared): "Yes, yes, hello, your majesty." The Snails were so frightened that they pulled their heads into their shells.
Crab (worried): "But Miss Sparrow flew by and told us that outside, it is very wide." Frog (angrily): "Lies! There is nothing outside this well at all!"
Day after day, Frog only ate, slept, croaked, and bragged. He would not listen to anyone, and he did not want to learn anything new.
Then one day, dark clouds filled the sky. Thunder rumbled, rain poured down in buckets, and the bamboo hedges swayed. Frog (surprised): "Oh my, what heavy rain!"
More and more rainwater poured into the well. The water rose and rose. Frog (scared): "The water is so high! I am being pushed up!" Crab (worried): "Frog, be careful!"
The water spilled over the mouth of the well, swept Frog outside, and set him down on the wet grass beside the village road. Frog (surprised): "Oh! Where in the world am I?"
The rain stopped. Frog looked around and saw that the sky today was much bigger. But he still would not believe it. Frog (proudly): "Hmph, the rain must have made the sky swell up. I am still the king!" So Frog strutted proudly out into the middle of the village road.
On the road, Ant was busily carrying a grain of rice bigger than his whole body. Frog (shouting): "Hey, you tiny creature, why don't you bow to the king?" Ant (happily): "Hello, Frog! I am busy carrying rice home to my nest, before it rains again."
Frog (surprised): "What is rice? There is nothing like that in my well." Ant (laughing): "Rice grows in the fields over there! A whole field of golden rice, wide and endless!"
Frog (laughing): "A field? I bet it is only as big as my well!" Ant (happily): "Go and see, and you will know."
Suddenly the ground began to shake. Clip-clop, clip-clop! A brown Horse galloped down the village road as fast as the wind. Horse (shouting): "Move aside! Move aside!" Frog (scared): "Oh! What a huge creature!"
Horse stopped quickly, only a tiny bit away from Frog. Horse (worried): "I almost stepped on you. Why are you sitting in the middle of the road?" Frog (scared): "I... I am the king." Horse (laughing): "Ha ha, what kind of king is so tiny?"
Horse (proudly): "I just ran all the way from the river dike to the village gate. Out there, there are markets, rivers, and so many other villages too." Frog (surprised): "Really? Is the world that wide?"
Just then, Uncle Buffalo came slowly home from the fields. On his back sat a little boy with a topknot, playing a flute. Uncle Buffalo (happily): "Children, what is all this noise about?"
Frog (worried): "Uncle, I came up from the well. I thought the sky was only as big as a pot lid, so why is it so big now?" Uncle Buffalo (laughing): "Ha ha, sitting at the bottom of a well, you could only see a tiny patch of sky."
Uncle Buffalo (happily): "Come, climb onto my back, and I will take you to the fields to see." Frog leaped onto Uncle Buffalo's back. Ant crawled up onto his horn, and Horse walked beside them.
When they reached the fields, Frog saw a whole sea of golden rice rustling in the wind. White storks fluttered by, and far away he saw the river dike and green bamboo hedges. Frog (surprised): "Wow, it is so wide! A thousand times wider than my well!"
Frog looked up. The blue sky stretched on and on, white clouds drifted lazily, and the children's kites soared high. Frog (surprised): "The sky is not a pot lid at all! It is so big, I cannot see where it ends!" Ant (laughing): "See? I told you so!"
Frog bowed his head, and his cheeks turned red. Frog (sadly): "All this time, I thought I was the king. It turns out I know so little." Frog (sadly): "I also shouted at my friends in the well. I am so ashamed."
Uncle Buffalo (happily): "Knowing that you do not know is already wiser. Everyone has things they do not know, dear child. We only need to work hard and learn."
Frog turned to Ant and Horse. Frog (sadly): "I was so rude earlier. Please forgive me, my friends." Ant (laughing): "It is all right, we are friends!"
Frog crossed his arms and bowed his head politely. Frog (firmly): "Uncle Buffalo, dear friends, please take me everywhere so that I can learn!" Horse (joyfully): "Of course! Tomorrow I will take you to the village market!" Ant (happily): "And I will show you how to find rice!"
In the days after that, Frog followed his friends everywhere. He visited the busy village market, stopped by the cool village pond, admired the temple yard, and even climbed up to the windy river dike. Frog (joyfully): "Today I learned something new again!"
One afternoon, Frog went back to the edge of the old well and called down to his friend. Frog (happily): "Crab, it is so wide out here! I was wrong before, and I am sorry!" Crab (joyfully): "How wonderful! Frog, tell me all about it!"
Frog sat on Uncle Buffalo's back and watched the red sunset glow over the fields. Frog (happily): "Thank you, Uncle Buffalo. You are my very first teacher!"
Little friends, from this story, our grandparents made a saying, a frog at the bottom of a well. When someone knows little but likes to brag, people still say it to this very day. In 2005, filmmakers used this story to make The Dream of the Green Frog, the first 3D animated film in Vietnam. The film ends with the words, Go and you will know this and that, stay in one place and you will never grow wise.
Uncle Buffalo led Frog out of his tiny sky the size of a pot lid. Your teachers do the same, every day opening a whole sky of knowledge for you. Vietnamese Teachers Day, on the twentieth of November, is coming soon. Remember to cross your arms and thank your teachers!
This story reminds us not to be proud like Frog was in his well. The world is very wide, so let us be humble and work hard to learn every day. If you liked today's story, please subscribe to the channel Huy Kể Chuyện to hear many more wonderful stories. See you again, friends!
